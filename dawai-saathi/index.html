How this works (for builders)

Dawai Saathi is an open agentic website. Its agent network is a public JSON file (dawai_saathi.network.json, agent-manifest.json) and runs in your browser. It reads brand names with a small sandboxed lookup tool, then calls the Jan Aushadhi Price Desk — another open agentic website that any pharmacy, hospital or NGO site can call — after you approve the contact. Your AI key goes only to the AI provider you choose, never to either site.

Important: Dawai Saathi gives price and generic-name facts only. It does not give medical advice. Never stop, start or change a medicine or dose without your doctor. Always show the list to your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

Prices: Pharmaceuticals & Medical Devices Bureau of India (PMBI), Product & MRP List, janaushadhi.gov.in, list dated 12 Nov 2026. Find a Kendra: Jan Aushadhi Sugam app · toll-free 1800-180-8080. Brand names belong to their owners and are used only to identify the salt. Independent project for the Open Agentic Web Challenge; not affiliated with PMBI.
